Foreign education

Do not pay for an evaluation before you know what is accepted.

Credential evaluation is not one-size-fits-all. The right evaluation can depend on the school, program, licensing authority, and purpose.

Get help with foreign education

Before you pay

  1. Choose your goal.
  2. Check what the school or licensing authority accepts.
  3. Determine whether you need document-by-document or course-by-course evaluation.
  4. Only then select an evaluation service.
Always confirm with the official school or licensing source before spending money.

Why Stepora exists

Built from lived experience.

Stepora was inspired by founder Itrat Batool's experience navigating U.S. higher education after coming to the United States from Pakistan.

A pathway that could have taken about four years ultimately took nearly a decade to navigate. Stepora is designed to give students the clear, practical roadmap she wished she had at the beginning.

“Students should not have to understand the entire education system before they can begin building their future. They only need to know their next step.
