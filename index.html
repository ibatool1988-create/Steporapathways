Foreign education

Do not pay for an evaluation before you know what is accepted.

Credential evaluation is not one-size-fits-all. The right evaluation can depend on the school, program, licensing authority, and purpose.

✓ Undergraduate admission ✓ Transfer credit ✓ Graduate admission ✓ Professional licensure ✓ Employment
Get help with foreign education

Before you pay

  1. Choose your goal.
  2. Check what the school or licensing authority accepts.
  3. Determine whether you need document-by-document or course-by-course evaluation.
  4. Only then select an evaluation service.
Stepora should always link students back to the official school or licensing source before they spend money.

Why Stepora exists

Built from lived experience.

Stepora was inspired by founder Itrat Batool's experience navigating U.S. higher education after coming to the United States from Pakistan.

A pathway that could have taken about four years ultimately took nearly a decade to navigate. Today, she works as a Clinical Laboratory Technologist Level 4 / Assistant Supervisor in Hematology at NYC Health + Hospitals/Kings County Hospital and is pursuing an MPH in Health Policy and Management at the CUNY Graduate School of Public Health & Health Policy.

Stepora is designed to give students the kind of clear, practical roadmap she wished she had at the beginning.

“ Students should not have to understand the entire education system before they can begin building their future. They only need to know their next step.
